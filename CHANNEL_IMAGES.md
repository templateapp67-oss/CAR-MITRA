## Hero prompt

Original wide cinematic photograph for the CarMitra Channel Partner Program. A bright, clean professional workshop with high-end tools and vehicles. Three diverse Indian professionals, a woman in a cream blazer and two men in yellow/blue CarMitra uniforms with photo ID, collaborate over a large screen displaying the CarMitra wordmark and a Jaipur map with luminous connections to 3 km zones. Forward-looking commercial photography in natural daylight, cream/white and yellow palette, subtle blue, people on the right with quiet left-side space for HTML copy, 2:1 landscape composition. No dark scene, unrelated logos, overlaid slogans, fake statistics or watermark.

## Zone card prompt

Premium white Zone Partner card on a cream desk, yellow accents and a stylized location pin with inward arrows. Text: “Zone Partner” and “Manage your local 3km radius territory with dedicated service partners.” Subtle glowing connections and miniature vehicles communicate local lead routing. Bright studio commercial photography, shallow depth of field, 3:2 landscape framing, centered card with safe cropping margins.

## City card prompt

Premium white City Partner card on a clean desk, Jaipur skyline integrated with a handshake, subtle growth graphs and network connections, blue/white with yellow accents. Text: “City Partner” and “Manage entire city operations and lead 50+ service partners.” Bright studio commercial photography, 3:2 composition, aspirational business model, no current-count guarantees.

## Equipment card prompt

Premium ivory Equipment Kit Partner card with toolbox, wrench and pressure-washer nozzle icon, yellow/white palette. Text: “Equipment Kit Partner” and “Create a stable income by supplying professional car & bike wash equipment to service partners.” Neat mobile service van, pressure washer, towels and supplies in the shallow background. Bright commercial photography, 3:2 composition, no extra claims or watermark.

## Application photograph prompt

Decorative bright flat-lay of cream-white application papers and a premium pen. Title: “Apply for Channel Partnership”. “Choose Your Partnership Model” card has Zone Partner checked. Headings: “Personal Information”, “Business Details”, “Available Investment Budget”; blank input lines and a printed yellow Submit button. Real paper texture, daylight, 3:2 landscape. No personal data. The website uses a separate functional HTML form.
